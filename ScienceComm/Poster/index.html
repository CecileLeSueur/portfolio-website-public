At large international conferences, hundreds of projects compete simultaneously for the attention of researchers. In this environment, a poster has only a few seconds to capture interest, spark curiosity, and convince people that a project is worth exploring further.

I designed this poster for the HUPO 20241 conference to introduce GPMelt [1], a statistical framework for Thermal Proteome Profiling (TPP) data analysis, to potential users ranging from experimental biologists to data scientists. Besides communicating the value of the framework, the poster was designed to attract attention, create a memorable impression, and encourage researchers to engage with the project beyond the poster session.

Challenges

At large international conferences, poster sessions are highly competitive environments in which hundreds of projects compete simultaneously for attention. Standing out from the crowd is therefore essential to attract an audience before they move on to the next poster, often leaving only a few minutes to pitch the project.

For GPMelt, this challenge was amplified by the nature of the project itself. Advanced statistical methodologies can appear intimidating to researchers unfamiliar with sophisticated modelling approaches, creating a barrier not only to understanding the work, but even to initially approaching the poster and engaging with the presenter.

The challenge was therefore to create a poster that attracted attention, lowered this initial barrier, and encouraged researchers from diverse backgrounds to engage confidently with both the project and the person presenting it.

Approach

I designed the poster as a self-contained narrative that combines visual impact, accessibility, and scientific credibility.

Key design choices included:

  • creating a strong visual identity through branding elements such as the software name, logo, and direct access to resources via QR codes;
  • creating a visually distinctive entry point (iceberg metaphor) designed to attract curiosity while making the underlying methodology feel more approachable;
  • structuring information in layers, allowing both quick scanning and deeper exploration depending on the audience’s level of interest;
  • highlighting the key features of the tool, its practical value, and its use cases through a clear structured layout;
  • designing the poster to function both as a conversation starter during the session and as a stand-alone communication resource afterwards.

Strengths

  • Visual storytelling for scientific engagement in a highly competitive environment;
  • Anticipating barriers to engagement and designing communication resources to overcome them;
  • Transforming intimidating technical topics into approachable conversations;
  • Creating curiosity around complex scientific and technical subjects;
  • Building memorable project identities through branding and communication design;
  • Translating methodological innovation into clear user value for diverse audiences;
  • Balancing clarity, completeness, and visual appeal in constrained communication formats.

Impact

The HUPO 2024 poster session proved particularly successful in attracting and maintaining engagement throughout the event. The poster consistently gathered a large audience and generated numerous discussions with researchers from diverse backgrounds.

Many participants with limited statistical training specifically commented on the accessibility of the explanations and their ability to grasp the core ideas behind the methodology. The combination of the poster design and the accompanying discussions helped create an environment in which researchers felt comfortable asking questions and engaging with concepts they might otherwise have considered too technical or intimidating.

Beyond the conference itself, the communication strategy succeeded in making both the project and the GPMelt framework memorable. Several researchers later recontacted me after the conference, demonstrating that the poster had not only captured attention during the session, but also left a lasting impression afterwards.
